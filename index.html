1. The starting move

Konstantin Meyl (Prof. Dr.-Ing., Furtwangen) does not start from the Heaviside–Hertz form of Maxwell. He starts from Faraday’s law of induction and asks what was dropped when the electric field was forced to be irrotational except for ∂B/∂t.

Textbook Faraday–Maxwell induction is

∇ × E = − ∂B / ∂t

Meyl’s dual extension adds a relaxation / vortex term with a time constant τ₂. He writes a potential density dual to current density:

b = B / τ₂
∇ × E = − ∂B/∂t − B/τ₂ = −μ (∂H/∂t + H/τ₂)

When τ₂ → ∞ the extra term vanishes and Maxwell is recovered. Finite τ₂ is the potential vortex of the electric field: a curl of E that is not produced by a changing B. That is the whole invention. Everything else — “scalar wave,” neutrino story, Tesla coil kit, Faraday-cage claim — is a reading of what those vortices do once they exist.

NASA/CR-2005-213749 (Alexander, MSE, Apr 2005), §3.4.3, is the standing survey of this line next to Valone and Puthoff US 5,845,220. Treat it as the institutional map, not as NASA endorsement of over-unity.

2. The fundamental field equation

From the extended induction law plus Ampère with a finite eddy-current time constant τ₁, Meyl obtains one vector PDE that is supposed to contain four pieces at once:

−c² ∇ × (∇ × E) = ∂²E/∂t² + (1/τ₁ + 1/τ₂) ∂E/∂t + E/(τ₁ τ₂)

Term by term, as he labels them:

  • Wave term — ordinary d’Alembert / Hertz piece, speed locked at c.
  • Eddy-current damping — τ₁, conducting media, skin effect.
  • Potential-vortex damping — τ₂, dielectrics and vacuum in his reading.
  • Poisson / source term — spatial distribution of currents and potentials.

He then splits the vector identity

∇²E = ∇(∇ · E) − ∇ × (∇ × E)

and reads the two pieces as two different waves:

v² ∇(∇ · B) − c² ∇ × (∇ × B) = ∂²B/∂t²

Left term: longitudinal / “scalar,” speed v not fixed. Right term: transverse Hertz, speed c fixed. Because ∇ · B is a scalar, he calls the longitudinal piece a scalar wave. That is a naming choice. It is not the same object as a mathematical scalar field φ(x,t) with no direction.

Plasma-wave reduction

If E=-∇φ, then ∇×E=0 by construction and the longitudinal branch becomes an inhomogeneous wave for the potential:

∇²φ − (1/c²) ∂²φ/∂t² = −ρ/ε

That is the ordinary Langmuir / plasma-wave equation. Meyl’s extra claim is that the same longitudinal structure survives in air and vacuum because the potential vortex itself plays the role of the “particle” that can shove the next particle — a Helmholtz ring vortex, not an electron gas.

4. Why this rhymes with Greenyer’s toroidal moment

Bob Greenyer’s “fractal toroidal moment” is not Meyl’s PDE. It comes from Zel’dovich’s anapole (1957) and Dubovik’s toroidal multipole (1965/67): a current that runs in poloidal loops around a torus so the magnetic moments cancel as a dipole and what remains is a toroidal moment T.

Tᵢ = (1/10c) ∫ [ rᵢ (r · J) − 2 r² Jᵢ ] d³r

Alignment, not identity:

  • Both treat a closed field vortex as the primitive object, not the Hertzian TEM pair.
  • Both want a configuration that can be field-quiet outside (anapole / non-radiating) while still carrying structure and momentum inside.
  • Both talk about nested / fractal vortices: Meyl’s potential vortex as particle; Greenyer’s super/hyper-toroidals as stacked T-moments.
  • Both look at Tesla-style coils, pancake windings, and plasma objects (EVOs, ball lightning, Shoulders) as engineering cousins of that geometry.

Where they split: Meyl writes an extended Maxwell PDE and a variable-speed scalar wave. Greenyer organizes condensed-matter / LENR morphology around a documented multipole (T) and around phase singularities. You can use Meyl as a wave-language for the same topology Greenyer draws. You should not paste Meyl’s over-unity or 1.5c claims into Dubovik’s T as if they were theorems.

Working hypothesis worth testing, not assuming: a bifilar or caduceus winding that Hively uses to launch a C ≠ 0, B ≈ 0 longitudinal E is geometrically a first-order toroidal-moment antenna. Meyl’s dual Tesla spheres are a cruder, higher-voltage version of the same topology.

6. How the sandbox computes SNR

Shared drive: current I into a 50 Ω equivalent, P_tx=½ I^{2}· 50. Shared intercept area A_e=0.1 m². Shared spreading 1/R^{2}. Noise is a flat floor N₀ in dBm (bandwidth already folded in). What changes is only the loss law each author tells.

SNR_dB = 10 log₁₀ (Pᵣ / N₀)

Classical TEM reference. Hull preset adds 3 cm of steel (σ∼ 10⁷ S/m) in front of seawater.

δ = √(2 / ωμσ) , L_skin = e^(−2R/δ)

Hively EED / SLW. B=0, C≠ 0, longitudinal E∥∇ C. No classical skin. Residual loss is weak conductivity damping, not 1/δ.

α_H ≈ 0.15 · (σ/2) √(μ/ε) , L_H = e^(−2 α_H R)

Nikolova–Zimmerman. Longitudinal A∥ still falls as 1/R. Metallic antennas miss most of it; plasma / biased charge coupling η∼ 0.05. Residual \alpha_{Z} is a bit harsher than Hively’s because they never claimed a skin-free conducting path — they claimed a different detector.

α_Z ≈ 0.4 · (σ/2) √(μ/ε) , L_Z = e^(−2 α_Z R)

Meyl vortex (claim model). Tunnel factor T∈[0,1] interpolates full skin and Hively-like residual. v/c raises effective tunneling and lowers \alpha_{M}. That encodes his contraction story without putting over-unity into Pᵣ.

T_eff = clip( T + 0.15(v/c − 1) , 0, 1 ) , α_M ≈ 0.08 · (σ/2) √(μ/ε) / (v/c)
L_M = e^(−2 α_M R) · ( T_eff + (1 − T_eff) e^(−2R/δ) )

8. What a careful EE should keep separate

Mathematics: Bruhn (TU Darmstadt) showed that if you demand both Meyl’s “scalar-wave condition” ∇×E=0 and source-free Maxwell in a homogeneous neutral medium, the only leftover solutions are not oscillating waves. Meyl’s reply is that the medium is not source-free because the vortex is the source. That is a modeling choice, not a derivation from Maxwell.

Experiment: LED-in-a-box and dual-resonance kits are easy to fool with common-mode RF, ground loops, and rectifier pickup. Hively-style S21 vs distance in a sealed can, and Zimmerman-style bias-reversed plasma, are harder tests. Use those as the bar.

Over-unity and 1.5c: recorded as Meyl claims. Not used as defaults in the sandbox energy budget. The v/c slider only changes his loss model, not special-relativistic kinematics.
